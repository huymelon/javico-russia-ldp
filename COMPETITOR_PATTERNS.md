## What top pages are doing well
- Use a very long-form SEO structure with many intent-matching sections.
- Put legal trust signals early: giấy phép, địa chỉ, hotline, website, văn phòng.
- Cover the exact queries users ask: điều kiện, hồ sơ, chi phí, lương, quy trình, cách chọn công ty uy tín.
- Repeat conversion CTAs throughout the page, not just once.
- Mix article depth with lead-generation elements.
- Use FAQ heavily to capture long-tail search intent.

## What JAVICO should copy in structure, not wording
- Intro paragraph that immediately states what the page will answer.
- Table-of-contents or “nội dung chính” block near the top.
- Clear H2s with keyword intent: điều kiện, hồ sơ, chi phí, lương, quy trình, uy tín.
- Trust/legal block showing the company is real and licensed.
- Comparison section that helps users decide between markets.
- Strong internal linking to pillar pages and official service pages.

## What JAVICO should avoid copying blindly
- Unsupported salary or cost numbers.
- Overclaiming safety, timelines, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Fake office locations, placeholder hotlines, or invented partner counts.
- Generic sales language like “việc nhẹ lương cao” or “100% đảm bảo”.
